- laskutuskone.fi: "ilmainen laskutusohjelma ja laskupohja" – free PDF template without signup as the top hook, plus a free software tier (customer register, invoice history) that funnels to paid plans ("Aloita ilmaiseksi" onboarding). Targets freelancers, small businesses and one-time private sellers.
- ilmainenlaskutusohjelma.fi: single-page free tool – no registration, logo upload, barcodes, "100% SEPA compatible", PDF output; thin content, dated presentation, heavy ad-monetization vibe.
- Neither leads with the 2026 VAT change (13,5%); both are FI-only.
