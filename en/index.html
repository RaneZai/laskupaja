FAQ

Is Laskupaja really free?

Yes. No signup, no payment, no usage caps. The tools run entirely in your browser.

Where is my data stored?

The invoice draft and settings are saved only in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is ever sent to a server, and you can wipe the saved data at any time.

Which VAT rate applies to my invoice?

It depends on the country of supply and what you sell – most services fall under the standard rate. See the country guides above for the current rates and a link to each tax authority.

Which currency does the generator use?

Euros only (€). Sweden and Poland use krona and złoty respectively – the guides for those countries explain how to convert amounts yourself.
