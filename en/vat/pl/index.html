VAT rates and invoicing in Poland

Poland (VAT) has a 23% standard rate, two reduced tiers – and 2026 is the year KSeF, the national e-invoicing system, becomes mandatory. Amounts are in złoty (PLN).

Current VAT rates

RateApplies to
23%Basic rate: most goods and services.
8%Reduced: e.g. certain foods, restaurant and hotel (accommodation) services, construction services in housing.
5%Further reduced: basic food products and books.
0%Exports, intra-EU supplies and a defined list of other operations – with both parties’ VAT numbers on the invoice.

From 1 January 2026 the subject-matter VAT exemption threshold rose from PLN 200,000 to PLN 240,000 of annual sales.

Polish VAT number (NIP)

The VAT number is PL + the 10-digit NIP, e.g. PL1234567890.

Show your VAT number on every invoice, and your customer’s VAT number when applying the intra-EU reverse charge. Check any EU VAT number in the Commission’s VIES service.

What an invoice must include

Under the EU VAT Directive every invoice needs at minimum: the supplier’s and customer’s name and identification numbers (VAT numbers for cross-border B2B), a sequential invoice number, the invoice date and date of supply, a description of the goods or services, the quantity and unit price, the net amount, and the VAT rate and amount per rate.

Worth knowing: KSeF becomes mandatory in 2026. Polish businesses must issue B2B invoices through the national e-invoicing system (Krajowy System e-Faktur, KSeF) from 1 February 2026 (largest taxpayers) and from 1 April 2026 for everyone else; 2026 runs as a transition year with no penalties for KSeF errors. A foreign supplier invoicing a Polish customer from abroad does not invoice through KSeF itself.
