VAT rates and invoicing in Spain

Spain (IVA) combines a 21% standard rate with two reduced tiers and a zero rate on a defined basket of basic foods. From 2026 the software you invoice with is itself regulated.

Current VAT rates

RateApplies to
21%General rate: most goods and services.
10%Reduced: e.g. passenger transport, hotels and restaurant service, some foodstuffs and health products.
4%Super-reduced: e.g. basic foods, books and newspapers, medicines.
0%A defined list of basic foodstuffs (bread, milk, eggs, fruit and vegetables, legumes…) plus intra-EU supplies and exports.

The tax agency publishes the exact lists per rate (see the Tipos IVA 2026 PDF linked in the footer).

Spanish VAT number (NIF)

The VAT number is the country code ES followed by the 9-character NIF: a letter + 7 digits + a control character for companies (e.g. ESB12345678), or 8 digits + a letter for individuals (e.g. ES12345678X).

Show your VAT number on every invoice, and your customer’s VAT number when applying the intra-EU reverse charge. Check any EU VAT number in the Commission’s VIES service.

What an invoice must include

Under the EU VAT Directive every invoice needs at minimum: the supplier’s and customer’s name and identification numbers (VAT numbers for cross-border B2B), a sequential invoice number, the invoice date and date of supply, a description of the goods or services, the quantity and unit price, the net amount, and the VAT rate and amount per rate.

Worth knowing: Veri*factu / regulated invoicing software. Under RD 1007/2023 (as amended by RD 254/2025), invoicing systems used in Spain must keep tamper-proof, traceable records, optionally reporting every invoice to the tax agency online (“VERI*FACTU” mode). The rules apply from 1 January 2026 for corporate income tax payers and from 1 July 2026 for the rest (SII filers are outside the scope). It binds the software you use in Spain – not the format of a paper/PDF invoice itself.

Currency

Spain’s currency is the euro (EUR) – the generator’s totals work as-is.
