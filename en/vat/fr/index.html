VAT rates and invoicing in France

France (TVA) runs one standard rate and three reduced tiers. Since September 2026 it is also the EU’s biggest e-invoicing rollout – plan for it if you invoice French businesses.

Current VAT rates

RateApplies to
20%Standard rate: most goods and services.
10%Intermediate: e.g. passenger transport, hotel accommodation, restaurant meals, renovation work.
5.5%Reduced: e.g. most foodstuffs, energy, books.
2.1%Super-reduced: medicines reimbursed by social security, and the press.
0%Intra-EU supplies and exports, with both parties’ VAT numbers on the invoice.

The exact scope is set by the Code général des impôts – check it for your own supply.

French VAT number

The numéro de TVA intracommunautaire is FR + a two-digit validation key + the 9-digit SIREN, e.g. FR40123456789. It is derived from the company’s SIREN registration.

Show your VAT number on every invoice, and your customer’s VAT number when applying the intra-EU reverse charge. Check any EU VAT number in the Commission’s VIES service.

What an invoice must include

Under the EU VAT Directive every invoice needs at minimum: the supplier’s and customer’s name and identification numbers (VAT numbers for cross-border B2B), a sequential invoice number, the invoice date and date of supply, a description of the goods or services, the quantity and unit price, the net amount, and the VAT rate and amount per rate.

Worth knowing: the e-invoicing reform is live. Since 1 September 2026 every French business must be able to receive structured e-invoices through approved platforms; large and mid-sized companies must also issue them (with e-reporting), and micro/small companies must issue from 1 September 2027. The obligation covers domestic B2B flows – invoices to foreign suppliers like a Finnish freelancer are not e-invoices, but related data may still need e-reporting by your client.
