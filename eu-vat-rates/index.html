Standard, reduced and special rates

Reduced and special rates apply to particular supplies under national rules. Check the classification of your goods or services before using one. Zero-rating and exemption also have different consequences for deducting input VAT.

Cross-border sales

The customer's country alone does not determine what VAT to charge. The type of sale, where it is taxed and the customer's status can change the treatment.
